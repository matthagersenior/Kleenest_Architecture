# Business Growth Capability Boundary

This boundary groups commercial/business-growth capabilities that share location identity and entitlement state.

## Canonical capability families

- campaigns
- contests
- events
- promotions
- QR / custom QR
- partnerships / partner programs
- search visibility boosts
- preferred locations
- engagement attribution
- occupancy / visitor metrics
- ROI / growth analytics
- business media
- business geofences

## Authority rule

Commercial state must be mutated through authoritative Business Supabase contracts. Analytics are derived consumers and must not become mutation authorities.

## Dependencies

Business identity → business membership/role → product entitlement → managed location → campaign/promotion/event/QR → engagement attribution → analytics/ROI → intelligence.
